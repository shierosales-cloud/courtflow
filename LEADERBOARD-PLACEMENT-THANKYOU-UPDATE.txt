CourtFlow v27 — Overall Leaderboard Position in Thank-You & Feedback

- The personalized post-session WhatsApp thank-you message now includes each player's Overall leaderboard position, captured at session end before session result counters are reset.
- Placement uses the same Overall leaderboard ranking and eligible-player count shown in the app at that moment. Top-three positions read, for example, "Top 3 of 32 players (3rd place)"; other positions show their ordinal rank and total. Ties are identified.
- The feedback link carries the placement to the player feedback page, which displays it and stores leaderboard_rank, leaderboard_total, leaderboard_tied and leaderboard_placement with the feedback submission.
- The organizer's post-session recap preview and Feedback dashboard also display the placement.
- Updated thank-you and feedback confirmation copy from a green heart to a blue heart and added the salute emoji.
- Netlify's static form blueprint was updated to declare the added hidden fields.
- Service-worker cache version bumped to force installed devices to fetch the updated page.

No database migration or new environment variables are required.
